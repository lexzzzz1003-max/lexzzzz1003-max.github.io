Undeclared Major · Photography

My name is Shazhou Zang, and I am a student at the University of California, Riverside. My major is currently undeclared, and I expect to graduate in 2030. I am using this stage of my education to explore my interests and consider the direction I want to take in the future. Photography is an important part of that exploration. It gives me a way to pay attention to the world, express a personal perspective, and connect what I see with what I want to communicate. This website introduces my educational path and brings together a selection of my photographs.

My interest in photography was part of my high school experience, when I participated in a photography club. That experience is a meaningful starting point for this portfolio. I enjoy the way a photograph can preserve an ordinary moment and make it worth another look. A mountain surrounded by clouds, a line of branches against the sky, or a small cluster of flowers can each become the subject of an image. The subjects may be different in scale, but they all offer opportunities to notice light, shape, color, and atmosphere.

Landscape and nature photography are especially appealing to me. In the photographs selected here, mountain ridges, changing skies, trees, and flowers offer different ways of looking at the natural world. Some images emphasize distance and open space, while others focus on smaller details. I like the contrast between these perspectives. Looking at a broad landscape can create a sense of scale, while looking closely at a flower or a branch can make a familiar subject feel more specific. Together, these photographs represent the kinds of scenes that attract my attention.

As an undeclared student, I want to keep learning before making a final decision about my major. I see this period as a chance to ask questions, try different subjects, and better understand the work that interests me. I also want to continue making time for photography alongside my education. Creative interests can provide another way to think about ideas, even when they are not part of a particular major. For me, keeping a portfolio is a useful way to bring an interest into a concrete form and share it with other people.

This resume website is also a learning project for my CS8 coursework. Working with a website template introduces another way to present photographs and personal information. My aim is to create a page that is clear, accessible, and easy to navigate. The experience section describes my photography club participation, personal photography, and this website project; the education section outlines my current academic direction. The portfolio below brings the visual work into the same space. As I move toward graduation in 2030, I hope to develop a clearer academic direction while continuing to explore the world through photography.
